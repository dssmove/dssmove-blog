Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This policy explains how Dssmove handles information on this blog (blog.dssmove.co.uk) and in the Dssmove YouTube Uploader, the tool we use to publish videos to our own YouTube channel. It doesn't cover the Dssmove property website and app at www.dssmove.co.uk; for questions about those, contact us below.

Who we are

Dssmove Limited runs Dssmove, a UK service helping people who receive benefits find homes to rent. Contact: dssmove@gmail.com.

This blog

The blog has no accounts, forms or comments, and we don't ask you for personal information. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) to run and protect the service. We don't use advertising cookies on the blog.

Dssmove YouTube Uploader

The Dssmove YouTube Uploader is an internal tool. It is used only by Dssmove, signed in with our own Google account, to upload and manage videos on the Dssmove YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public and does not collect information about anyone else.

  • What it accesses: our own YouTube channel, through the YouTube API Services, to upload videos, set titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, captions, playlists and release times, and to check a video's status.
  • How it's stored: the sign-in token is kept only on our own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • Removing access: access can be withdrawn at any time from Google Account permissions.

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using YouTube you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask what information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Email dssmove@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
